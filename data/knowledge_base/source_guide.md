# Multi-Domain Knowledge Base Source Guide

This guide lists practical sources you can collect for each domain in StartupSpark AI.

## 1. Common Startup Sources

Use these across all domains:

- Y Combinator Startup Library
- Product Hunt launch pages
- SBA business guides
- Harvard Business Review startup articles
- first-round and a16z blogs
- Business model and validation frameworks
- public pitch deck examples
- startup failure case studies

## 2. Healthcare AI

Good sources:

- WHO reports
- CDC publications
- NHS or public hospital digital health reports
- healthcare AI company blogs
- medical device and telehealth case studies
- PubMed review papers
- health-tech market reports
- hospital workflow and patient triage use cases

Search queries:

- healthcare ai market report pdf
- telemedicine trends pdf
- patient triage ai case study
- medical workflow automation report

## 3. FinTech

Good sources:

- World Bank financial inclusion reports
- IMF and central bank publications
- Stripe, Wise, PayPal, Revolut blogs
- fintech startup case studies
- payment and lending product pages
- open banking explainers
- fraud detection whitepapers
- neobank market reports

Search queries:

- fintech market report pdf
- digital payments report pdf
- open banking whitepaper
- fraud detection ai case study

## 4. EdTech

Good sources:

- UNESCO education reports

- Coursera, Duolingo, Khan Academy blogs
- LMS and learning analytics case studies
- remote learning trend reports
- student engagement research
- AI tutor product pages
- university digital learning papers

- edtech trends report pdf
- AI tutor case study
- learning analytics whitepaper
- student engagement report

## 5. AgriTech

## 6. Cybersecurity

Good sources:

- CISA advisories
- NIST publications
- ENISA reports
- security vendor blogs
- threat intelligence reports
- compliance guides

Search queries:

- cybersecurity trends report pdf
- zero trust whitepaper

- iam case study

## 7. What to Collect for Each Domain

Aim for 5 to 10 strong files per domain:

- 1 market report
- 1 competitor analysis
- 1 or 2 case studies
- 1 technical or research paper
- 1 product/pricing page summary
- 1 regulation or compliance note if relevant
- 1 manually written summary

## 8. Quality Rules

- Prefer public and recent sources
- Save the original URL in metadata
- Use one file per source when possible
- Remove noisy content before chunking
- Keep domain tags consistent

## 9. Simple Metadata Template

```json
{
  "domain": "fintech",
  "title": "Digital Payments Report 2025",
  "source": "World Bank",
  "url": "https://example.com",
  "document_type": "pdf",
  "publish_date": "2025-01-01",
  "trust_level": "high",
  "summary": "High-level notes about the document"
}
```
